PONS LAUNCHPAD MECHANICS

How A Pons Launch Actually Works

Pons is a bonding-curve launchpad. Buyers trade against a formula rather than an order book: each purchase moves the price up along the curve, each sale moves it back down. If enough is bought to reach the platform's graduation threshold, Pons migrates the accumulated liquidity into a Uniswap V3 pool and locks that LP position. If the threshold is never reached, the token simply stays on the curve.

If Graduation Happens LP Locked

The four stages of a Pons launch. This describes the mechanism — it is not a claim about where $HOODIE currently stands.

1 · Creation Token deployed, curve opens to everyone at the same price
2 · Open Trading Anyone can buy or sell against the curve at any moment
3 · Graduation Threshold Reached only if enough net buying occurs — it may never happen
4 · Uniswap V3 + LP Lock Liquidity migrates and the LP position is locked by the protocol
WHAT THIS ACTUALLY IS

Hoodie, Explained Honestly

Four things worth knowing before you decide anything.

There Is A Creator Fee

A 2.0% creator fee is charged on trades and goes to the token creator's wallet, on top of the trade fee Pons charges for itself. That fee is paid by the people trading. We say so up front because it is the main way the creator makes money from this token, and you should factor it into anything you do.

Fair Launch, No Reserved Supply

There is no pre-sale, no team allocation and no vesting cliff. Everyone — including the creator — buys from the same curve at the same price. The creator may make an opening buy like any other participant; if so, those tokens can be sold like anyone else's.

LP Lock Is Not Total Safety

On graduation, Pons locks the Uniswap V3 LP position, which means that specific pool cannot be withdrawn by the creator. It does not mean the price is protected. Any holder — creator included — can still sell their own tokens at any time, and the price can fall to near zero.

A Nod, Not An Endorsement

The name and the fox riff on the folk tale of Robin Hood and on retail-trader culture. $HOODIE runs on Robinhood Chain because that is a public network anyone can deploy to. It is not made, backed, reviewed or endorsed by Robinhood Markets, Inc.
